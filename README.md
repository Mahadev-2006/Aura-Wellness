# Aura Wellness 2026 🧘✨

**Aura Wellness** is a premium, AI-powered mental health platform designed for the year 2026. Inspired by industry leaders like Amaha Health, Aura blends clinical excellence with futuristic technology to provide a predictive, compassionate, and hyper-personalized wellness ecosystem.

## 🚀 Vision 2026
In 2026, mental healthcare is proactive. Aura uses advanced AI to forecast emotional energy, integrates seamlessly with neural-interfaced wearables, and ensures data sovereignty through decentralized storage.

## ✨ Key Features
- **Predictive AI Mood Forecasting**: Visualizing emotional energy outlooks based on biological rhythms and calendar data.
*   **Interactive AI Companion**: A "Talk to Aura" voice-first interface with a real-time chatbot assistant.
- **Evidence-Based Care**: Comprehensive modules for Anxiety, Depression, Sleep, Relationships, Work Stress, and Self-Esteem.
- **Clinical Aesthetics**: A warm, professional UI designed for trust, clarity, and accessibility.
- **Multi-Page Architecture**: Dedicated sections for Programs, Clinical Approach, and Support.

## 🛠️ Technology Stack
- **Frontend**: Semantic HTML5, Vanilla CSS3 (Custom Design System).
- **Interactivity**: Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+), Intersection Observer API for scroll animations.
- **Icons**: FontAwesome 6.0.
- **Typography**: Google Fonts (Outfit & Plus Jakarta Sans).

## 📂 Project Structure
- `index.html`: Homepage & Mood Forecasting.
- `about.html`: Mission & Clinical Methodology.
- `services.html`: Programs & Membership Plans.
- `concerns.html`: Detailed Clinical Encyclopedia.
- `contact.html`: Support Hub & FAQ.
- `login.html`: Secure User Portal.
- `aura_style.css`: Unified Design System.
