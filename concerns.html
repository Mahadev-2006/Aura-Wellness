Understanding Your Wellbeing

Knowledge is the first step toward healing. Explore detailed information about common mental health concerns and how our specialized care can help you find balance.

Anxiety & Panic

Anxiety is more than just feeling stressed. it can involve persistent worry, physical tension, and a racing heart. It often affects daily tasks and social interactions.

Common Symptoms:

  • Restlessness and inability to relax
  • Difficulty concentrating
  • Persistent feeling of dread
  • Physical symptoms like sweating or trembling
How Aura Helps: We use Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) modules to help you identify triggers and teach real-time grounding techniques through our AI companion.

Depression

Depression can feel like an overwhelming weight that makes it difficult to find joy in things you once loved. It affects your energy, sleep, and overall perspective on life.

Common Symptoms:

  • Persistent low mood or sadness
  • Loss of interest in hobbies
  • Changes in appetite or weight
  • Feelings of worthlessness or guilt
How Aura Helps: Through daily mood journaling and behavioral activation tasks, Aura helps you rebuild small, positive habits and tracks your emotional progress over time.

Sleep Issues

Poor sleep quality can significantly impact your mental clarity and emotional resilience. Whether it's insomnia or inconsistent cycles, sleep is vital for mental health.

Common Symptoms:

  • Difficulty falling asleep
  • Waking up frequently during the night
  • Waking up too early and not being able to sleep again
  • Feeling tired or irritable the next day
How Aura Helps: We provide personalized sleep hygiene routines and digital wind-down exercises that help quiet the mind before bed.

Relationship Dynamics

Our connections with others are fundamental to our happiness. Issues in relationships—whether romantic, familial, or professional—can lead to deep emotional distress.

Common Symptoms:

  • Constant conflict or communication breakdown
  • Feelings of isolation within a relationship
  • Difficulty setting or maintaining boundaries
  • Trust issues and emotional distance
How Aura Helps: Aura offers guided communication exercises and conflict-resolution frameworks designed to build empathy and strengthen emotional bonds.

Workplace Stress & Burnout

In today's fast-paced world, work stress can easily spill over into your personal life. If left unmanaged, it can lead to physical and emotional burnout.

Common Symptoms:

  • Chronic fatigue and lack of energy
  • Decreased productivity and focus
  • Feelings of detachment from your job
  • Physical symptoms like headaches or muscle tension
How Aura Helps: We provide time-management optimization and stress-reduction micro-tasks that fit into your busy workday to prevent burnout before it starts.

Self-Esteem & Confidence

How we view ourselves dictates how we interact with the world. Low self-esteem can prevent us from reaching our full potential and enjoying meaningful experiences.

Common Symptoms:

  • Persistent negative self-talk
  • Sensitivity to criticism
  • Avoidance of new challenges due to fear of failure
  • Constant social comparison
How Aura Helps: Aura uses daily affirmations, confidence-building tasks, and perspective-shifting modules to help you rewrite your internal narrative.
